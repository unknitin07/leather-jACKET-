Support & FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I care for my leather jacket? ▼
Wipe your jacket with a soft, damp cloth regularly. Condition with our premium leather conditioner every 6 months. Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and heat sources. Our leather develops a beautiful patina over time with proper care.
What is your return policy? ▼
We offer a 60-day return policy on all items. If you're not completely satisfied, simply initiate a return from your account. We'll provide a prepaid shipping label. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days.
How long does shipping take? ▼
We offer free express shipping on all orders, arriving in 2-3 business days. International orders typically arrive within 7-10 business days. You'll receive a tracking number via email once your order ships.
Is your leather ethically sourced? ▼
Absolutely. All our leather comes from certified tanneries that adhere to strict environmental and ethical standards. We're proud to support responsible leather production practices globally.
Do you offer customization? ▼
Yes! We offer custom alterations, personalization, and bespoke leather jacket services. Contact our support team to discuss your specific requirements and receive a custom quote.
